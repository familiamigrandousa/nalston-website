Nalston Strategic Group LLC

This policy explains how Nalston Strategic Group LLC (“Nalston,” “we,” “our”) handles information associated with this website and business inquiries.

Information you provide

When you email us, we receive your email address and any name, company details, contact information or message content you choose to share. Please send only information relevant to your inquiry.

The inquiry composer

The optional contact form prepares an email draft on your device. It does not submit information to a website server or save it in browser storage. Selecting “Prepare email” passes the draft to your email application; selecting “Copy inquiry” writes it to your device’s clipboard. Information reaches us only when you send an email.

Website hosting and cookies

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to provide and protect its hosting service. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. This website does not add analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. Images, styles and scripts are served with the website; no external font service is used.

How we use information

We use information shared in inquiries to respond, assess potential commercial relationships, follow up on discussions and maintain relevant business records.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by providers supporting our email, hosting and business operations, or disclosed when required by applicable law. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We keep inquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to handle the inquiry, maintain relevant business records and meet applicable obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no communication or storage system is completely secure.

Your requests

You may contact us to ask about your information or request access, correction or deletion. We will consider requests in accordance with applicable requirements and any records we need to retain. Email nicolas@nalstongroup.com.

Updates

We may update this policy as our website or business practices change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
